How it works

1

We shoot

The camera is connected straight to a phone. Every frame is saved and sent to our server the moment it's taken.

2

Guests scan the QR

One link per event. The gallery updates itself, so new photos appear without a refresh.

3

Guests share too

Guests can upload photos from their own phones. Images are compressed on the phone first, so uploads are quick.
