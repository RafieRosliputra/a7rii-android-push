How it works

No app for your guests to install. One link, one QR code.

1

We shoot

Our camera sends every photo straight to your private event gallery during the event.

2

Guests scan

Put the QR code on tables, screens or the backdrop. The gallery opens in any phone browser.

3

Everyone shares

Guests watch new photos appear live, download their favourites and upload their own shots.
